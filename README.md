## About

I build systems at the intersection of **technical delivery, AI governance, cloud/service operations and applied analytics**. My background spans SAP cloud delivery and AIOps initiatives, IBM/Kyndryl enterprise IT service operations, applied AI/ML research, and independent product development.

I am especially interested in how technical systems become operationally useful: **clear ownership, measurable controls, reliable workflows, explainable decisions, safe automation and evidence that teams can act on**.

## Featured work

### 🛡️ [AutonomousOps — AI Incident Governance & Service Recovery](https://github.com/Samadritaacharya/autonomousops-incident-response-agent)

Event-driven incident response with deterministic risk controls, grounded runbook retrieval, human approval gates, simulated remediation, stakeholder communication and auditable AgentOps.

- 24 labeled synthetic incident scenarios with CI-verified severity, runbook and approval-gate checks
- local TF-IDF cosine retrieval with deterministic service affinity
- optional LLM hypotheses separated from operational authority
- GitHub Issues → Actions event path with authorized `/approve` and `/reject` handling
- Next.js command center, FastAPI, Streamlit, Docker and cross-stack parity tests

**[View source →](https://github.com/Samadritaacharya/autonomousops-incident-response-agent)**

### ⭐ [PlatformPulse — Developer Platform Product Lab](https://github.com/Samadritaacharya/platformpulse-developer-platform)

An end-to-end developer-platform product case connecting **developer discovery, secure golden paths, service ownership, CI/CD and SLO metrics, A/B experimentation, AI governance, reliability and evidence-based roadmap decisions**.

- 9 product views spanning discovery → delivery → measurement → governance
- secure-by-default service generator with Docker, Kubernetes, Helm and CI artefacts
- synthetic platform telemetry, experiment evidence and service-health analytics
- automated QA, browser validation, static security analysis and dependency scanning
- interactive product experience plus the complete Streamlit implementation

**[Open live product →](https://samadritaacharya.github.io/platformpulse-developer-platform/)** · **[View source →](https://github.com/Samadritaacharya/platformpulse-developer-platform)**
